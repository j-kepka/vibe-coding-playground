## Integration and preservation

The original MP3 recordings were downloaded directly from the author on
**2026-10-09**. Their bytes, audio and embedded metadata are unchanged; only
their local filenames differ. No recordings were trimmed, normalized, remixed
or re-encoded. In the game, the recordings accompany moving scenes and may
repeat; playback volume, pauses and transitions are controlled at runtime.
These game contributions do not replace the recordings' CC BY 4.0 licence.

Codex checked the current track-page attribution against the author's
[catalog](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/pieces.json), matching each
title, original filename and ISRC. The [provenance
record](assets/music/provenance.json) contains download URLs, byte counts,
SHA-256 hashes and the supplied metadata notices. Earlier versions or mirrors
may mention CC BY 3.0; this distribution uses the author's current CC BY 4.0
offer linked above.

When redistributing the music, preserve the author, source, licence and
supplied notices; indicate any later modifications. Do not add legal or
technical restrictions that prevent recipients from exercising the
recordings' CC BY 4.0 rights. No author endorsement is implied.
