# Replacement game title — preliminary screening

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-10  
**Scope:** Indexed web searches and selected primary-source pages; candidate selection, not trademark clearance

**Subsequent decision:** I selected **Wandmoss Journey** on 2026-10-10 and
asked Codex to apply it throughout the game and current documentation. The
rename is recorded in [verification](../VERIFICATION.md#wandmoss-journey-branding-and-save-compatibility--2026-10-10).
The screening below records the evidence available before that decision;
The subsequent [official-register review](WANDMOSS_TRADEMARK_REVIEW.md) is now
complete within its stated scope and access/coverage limits; this preliminary
screening alone did not establish that result.

I decided to use one English public title across the English, German and
Polish interfaces. I proposed **Moosforest Journey** and **Moonforest Journey**
after my own searches suggested they might be original. Codex checked these
proposals and alternatives. At this screening stage I had not yet selected a
replacement and the previous branding remained in place.

## Candidates and recommendation

| Candidate | Finding in the results reviewed | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Moosforest Journey** | No exact full-title game match identified; no relevant game-title use of Moosforest or Moos Forest identified. | An option to consider, but “moss” is the English spelling for the plant; “moos” can read as a spelling error in an English title. |
| **Moonforest Journey** | No exact full-title game match identified. Existing games use Moon Forest. | Codex recommends avoiding it for distinctiveness and searchability; this is not a finding that its use would necessarily infringe a right. |
| **Mossforest Journey** | No exact full-title game match identified. Mossforest is also used in an existing nature-recording album title. | The English spelling correction of the first proposal; less distinctive as a root than a newly coined name. |
| **Wandmoss Journey** | The exact full-title query returned empty results. No relevant game or brand use of Wandmoss was identified in the reviewed root searches; a store-domain query also returned empty results. | Codex's preferred candidate: a coined combination of wand and moss, with Journey describing the four-chapter adventure. |
| **Cloudward Wand** | No exact full-title game match identified. The store-domain query returned empty results. | A shorter alternative connecting the wizard's wand with the cloud chapter. |

These assessments are naming recommendations, not proof of worldwide
uniqueness or legal availability.

## Relevant primary sources

- EYOUGAME's [Moon Forest listing on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.eyougame.yye.th)
  identifies the game in its English description as Moon Forest:Vertical RPG.
- GooseKult publishes [Midnight Madness at Moon Forest](https://goosekult.itch.io/midnight-madness-at-moon-forest)
  on itch.io. Its title and description were directly checked.
- The [Moon Forest restaurant](https://moon-forest.jp/en/) uses the root
  commercially outside games. This does not by itself prohibit a game title.
- Listening Earth's [Mossforest — New Guinea](https://listeningearth.com/play/album/89Mossforest.php)
  is an album, not an identified game-name collision.
- [moon-forest on itch.io](https://moon-forest.itch.io/) and
  [moonforest890's profile](https://itch.io/profile/moonforest890) surfaced as
  creator/user identifiers, not games entitled Moonforest. The former creator
  page could not be opened through the browsing tool; only its indexed result
  was available in this review.

Joining Moon Forest into one word does not eliminate the visible similarity
to those existing game titles. Codex's preference for another root follows
from these observed uses, rather than an inferred trademark registration.

## Search record

Codex used these candidate searches:

- Exact queries `"Moosforest Journey"`, `"Moonforest Journey"` and
  `"Mossforest Journey"`, also repeated with `game` appended.
- Roots `"Moosforest"`, `"Moos Forest"`, `"Moonforest"`, `"Moon Forest"`
  and `"Mossforest"`, each with `game` and individually with
  `site:store.steampowered.com`, `site:itch.io` and
  `site:play.google.com/store/apps`.
- General searches `"Moosforest"`, `"Moos Forest"`, `"Mossforest"`,
  `"Moonforest" official` and `"Mossforest" "Listening Earth"`.
- `"Wandmoss Journey"`, `"Wandmoss"`, `"Wandmoss" trademark`,
  `"Cloudward Wand"` and `"Cloudward Wand" game`.
- `"Wandmoss" (site:store.steampowered.com OR site:itch.io OR site:play.google.com)`
  and the same store-domain query with `"Cloudward Wand"`.

Initial grouped store searches for the owner-proposed roots returned empty
results and were followed by the individual queries above. Some quoted web
queries produced fuzzy or unrelated results instead of exact phrase matches;
Codex inspected the returned titles rather than treating every result as a
collision. Wandmoss appeared in unrelated OCR text and word co-occurrences,
which did not identify a relevant game or brand.

Other exploratory queries covered Mosswhisk, Wandermoss, Mossbound Skies,
Moonmoss Journey, Moss & Moonboots, Mosswisp Journey, Moonfern Journey,
Bramblewick Skies and Mossward Journey (including `"Mossward" game`). Codex
did not retain these on the recommended shortlist: several roots had existing
creative uses or gaming associations, and two clearer candidates were enough
for this selection step. They were not individually cleared or exhaustively
reviewed.

## Limits and next step

This search does not cover every live storefront catalogue, unindexed or
unpublished games, all spelling/phonetic variants, domain availability or
social handles. No live trademark register was queried for these replacement
candidates. A general search with the word “trademark” is not a substitute
for a registry search; empty results establish only that no matching result
was found in that query.

The subsequent [register review](WANDMOSS_TRADEMARK_REVIEW.md) checked the
full title, distinctive root and stated similar spellings, considering
software/game and online-entertainment categories. Both that bounded review
and the owner-authorized branding change are complete; publication remains
pending, and the review's limits still apply. Preserve the
[earlier current-name and asset audit](NAME_AND_LICENSE_REVIEW.md) as a record
of the previous names; its licence findings do not clear a replacement title.
